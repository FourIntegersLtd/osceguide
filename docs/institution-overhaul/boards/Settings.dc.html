Steps
  1. Each tab on this board is one screen or state. Build every one; none is optional.
  2. Each button that opens a box is a Modal (or ConfirmModal for a yes/no). Open each box on this board to see its words.
  3. Copy every word on the board exactly, including the empty, error and busy texts. Put nouns through useAssessWords so hire reads its own words.
  4. Find the button's call in the list above, by its summary. Today's caller (the "Now:" file) already makes that call: reuse its function from that file's api.ts. Do not write a new fetch.
  5. Show a write button only to the person in "Who sees it". Anyone else gets the same screen with no write buttons.
  6. Write a failing test first (Testing Library, assert the literal words), then build, then run npm run ci.
  7. Live data comes from the existing SSE streams (useMeEvents or the */stream route above). Never poll.
  8. Nothing that exists today may disappear. If a control on today's screen is not on this board, stop and ask.

Set up two-factor sign-in

After setup, every sign-in needs your password and a 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

  1. Open an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password and scan this code.
    Setting up on the phone that has the authenticator app? Tap Open in your authenticator app instead of scanning.
  2. Or add an account by hand with this setup key: JBSW Y3DP EHPK 3PXP
  3. Enter the six-digit code the app shows.

Your recovery codes

Keep these somewhere safe. Each one signs you in once if you lose your authenticator. This is the only time we will show them.

New recovery codes

Your current recovery codes stop working as soon as new ones are made.

Turn off two-factor sign-in?

You will sign in with your password alone. Your recovery codes stop working. Enter a code from your authenticator app to continue.

Delete your account?

Here is what happens.

  • Now: you are signed out, paid access ends with the account, and there is no refund.
  • For 30 days: sign in again to reopen the account.
  • After 30 days: your details, attempts and feedback are erased.

Delete your account?

This cannot be undone after 30 days.
